CLONE ME (AI Expert, Module 5, Lesson 2)

WHAT YOU WILL BUILD
A live camera app. Press SPACE, wait for 3-2-1, and the AI freezes a cut-out copy of you. Walk somewhere else in the
picture: now you are there twice. Freeze more copies, then press s to save one photo with all of them.

THE AI
MODNet, a small AI model (modnet.onnx) trained on photos of people. For every pixel it answers "how much of this pixel
is a person?" with a number from 0 to 1. That map of numbers is called a matte. It runs on your own computer.

YOUR FILE
clone_me.py. Lines marked (given) are done for you: read their notes A to H. You write TODO 1 to TODO 4, in order.
The TODO comments explain each job in words. Run the program after each TODO and check the result.

STEP 1: Run the starter (setup: see SETUP.txt)
Press SPACE and wait for 3-2-1. The help line says "1 copies", but no copy appears, and the terminal says
"Copy 1: you fill 0% of the picture". Why? Read cut_out().

STEP 2: TODO 1, cut_out(): get the matte from the AI
Shrink the frame, change BGR to RGB as float32, scale to -1..1, reshape to (1, 3, height, width), run the model,
and size its answer back to the frame.
Check: press SPACE. The terminal says "you fill" more than 0%. (The copy still does not show: that is TODO 2.)

STEP 3: TODO 2, paste(): blend the person onto the scene
Matte 1 = the person's pixel, 0 = the scene's pixel, in between = a mix.
Check: freeze a copy and walk to the other side: your copy stays in the picture.

STEP 4: TODO 3: the live you in front
Paste the live you last, after the frozen copies.
Check: walk in front of a copy. You pass in front of it.

STEP 5: TODO 4, final_photo(): the saved photo
Build it from the frozen copies only: the newest copy's frame, the older copies on it, and the newest you on top.
Check: freeze 2 or 3 copies, press s, and open the clone_photo file in this folder: every copy of you is in it.

STEP 6: Find what the AI cannot do
Freeze with only a toy in view. Freeze with your hand over your face. Freeze with someone else in view.
What happens each time, and why?

KEYS
SPACE = 3-2-1 then freeze a copy (holding it counts once)    s = save (needs 2 or more copies)
u = undo the last copy    c = clear all copies    q or Esc = quit (or close the window)

TIPS
Keep the laptop still between copies. Sit about an arm's length from the camera. Keep your hands off your face for a
pose. Errors appear in the terminal, not in the camera window.

SHOW YOUR PARENT
Make a photo with three of you, and say: "The AI marks every pixel as me or not me, and my code pastes the copies
together."
